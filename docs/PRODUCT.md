# Product

SAL DA TERRA V1 lets authorized ministry leaders manage adolescents, EBD events and attendance, view transparent participation indicators, and record pastoral follow-ups. Students see only their own permitted profile and attendance.

Success means a leader can answer within one minute: membership totals, last-Sunday attendance, repeated absences, individual frequency, attention queue, last follow-up, and monthly EBD evolution.

## Language boundary

Metrics describe observable engagement, participation, progress, journey, and follow-up. They do not measure faith, holiness, communion with God, or absolute spiritual maturity.

## V1 exclusions

No chat, social feed, public profiles/rankings, AI, full courses, gamification, certificates, payments, native app, or push notifications.
